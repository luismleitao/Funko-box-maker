Funko Box Maker v2 · bottom panel + tuck flaps

Enter the box size, add the five pictures, then print the net at actual size.

1. Units & paper

Changing the measurement system also switches to the matching default paper. You can change it again below.

2. Box size (outside measurements)

Width = front to back edge, Depth = side to side, Height = bottom to top. Use the outside size of the finished box.

3. Pictures

Each picture is auto-fitted to its panel. Fit panel (whole picture) shows the entire image inside the panel (default). Fill panel (crop edges) covers the whole panel and crops the overflow. Stretch to panel fills the panel edge to edge with no crop, but changes the picture's proportions. Use Rotate if a picture is sideways.

Print layout (cut & fold guide)

Cut Fold
  1. In the print dialog set Scale to 100% (or "Actual size") and turn off Fit to page.
  2. On page 1 check the scale bar at the bottom measures exactly the length printed next to it.
  3. Cut along the solid lines. Fold along the dashed lines.
  4. Each page overlaps the next by 12 mm. Line up the panel edges in the overlap strip, then tape from the inside.
  5. Glue the tab on the back panel to the inside of the left side panel to close the box.
